About NOVAPATH

Supporting innovation from concept to delivery.

NOVAPATH ADVISORY LTD is a UK-based advisory and management consultancy supporting technology, research and innovation projects.

We work with businesses, technical specialists and international partners to develop structured project plans, coordinate collaborative programmes, manage compliance requirements and prepare projects for implementation and commercial growth.

Our approach combines practical project management with commercial, administrative and strategic support.
